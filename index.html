A LETTER I ALMOST DIDN'T SEND

For You 🤍

There was a time when you weren't just someone I knew.

You were someone I looked for in a crowd, someone whose message could change the mood of an entire day, someone whose presence slowly became part of my life.

Maybe life changed. Maybe we changed. Maybe some chapters simply aren't meant to stay the same forever.

But changing doesn't make the memories disappear.

So today, I don't want to ask for anything. I don't want to reopen anything. I only wanted to leave one quiet wish for someone who once meant — and in some quiet corner of my heart, still means — a lot.
